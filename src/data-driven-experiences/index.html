The Existing Model is Not Fit For Purpose

The current privacy model is driven by an open market where the best ideas prevail, operating with limited regulation and poor transparency. While this model fosters rapid innovation driven by profitability and market demand, it leaves both service providers and customers exposed to unacceptable risks, a lack of efficiency, and stifles market adoption.

Market-Driven Innovation with limited regulation encourages creativity and the development of new services. However, without adoption of established industry standards, it has also led to a highly fragmented Ecosystem, where multiple standards and practices lead to an abuse of data, that erodes user trust. This creates market adoption barriers, where users are more hesitant to adopt new services that require extensive personal data, such as personalisation and facial recognition technologies.

The Solution: Trust3.0's "Privacy by Design"

In the very near future, we will be able to offer and consume incredibly rich and personalised services, enabled by AI, that were almost unthinkable only a few years ago. This currently comes at a cost. Whether you are a start-up just making inroads into a nascent market, or an established major player, offering these services without strong data privacy frameworks in place could have an immeasurable impact on your future success. The companies that recognise and implement the required changes now will be the ones that flourish. Which is better for you, and better for society.


Trust 3.0 has developed a simple step-by-step “Privacy by Design” methodology, to offer a practical solution for organisations that want to address the privacy challenge head on. The first three stages are technology agnostic, focussing on the form and function of the privacy requirements, in order to implement the right solution and to reap the benefits.
